# Cold Email Sequences — Sleep Lab / Sleep Center Owners

**Target:** Owner / Medical Director / Administrator of independent sleep labs and sleep centers
**Offer:** Physician referral pipeline (cold email outreach to primary care physicians, pulmonologists, and cardiologists on their behalf)
**Smartlead setup:** Day 1 → Day 4 → Day 9 | 40/day | pause on reply

---

## Email 1 — Day 1

**Subject:** how new physicians find {{company}}

Hi {{first_name}},

Quick question — when a primary care physician who just joined a practice in your area needs to refer a patient for a sleep study, how do they know to call {{company}}?

Most sleep lab directors I ask say: the docs sending us patients have been sending us for years. New ones? They don't know we exist.

Which means your referral volume is entirely dependent on relationships that were built a long time ago and don't grow on their own.

Physician turnover is constant — new PCPs enter every market every quarter, and hospital systems are actively working to route those new physicians toward their own labs. Whoever reaches a new physician first typically gets the referral habit.

We run outreach to primary care physicians, pulmonologists, and cardiologists in your geography on behalf of {{company}} — introducing the lab and getting on their radar before someone else does.

Typically adds 5–8 net-new referring physicians in the first 90 days.

Worth a 15-minute call?

{{signature}}

## Email 2 — Day 4

**Subject:** the AASM credentialing angle

{{first_name}} —

If {{company}} is AASM-accredited, you have a significant advantage over independent labs that aren't — physicians trust accredited facilities, and many hospital credentialing committees specifically recommend AASM labs when ordering out.

But that advantage only matters if the physicians in your area actually know about it.

Most AASM-accredited labs assume the credential speaks for itself. It doesn't if no one's heard of you.

We lead with the accreditation in every outreach message — it's the credibility hook that gets a new PCP to respond where a generic introduction wouldn't. "Board-certified sleep physicians, AASM-accredited facility, results back within 48 hours" is a very different message than "we do sleep studies."

For labs that aren't yet accredited, we use the physician relationships we build to make the case for pursuing it — it's a compounding advantage.

15 minutes to walk through how we'd structure it for {{company}}?

{{signature}}

## AASM Directory Supplement

The American Academy of Sleep Medicine maintains a public directory of accredited sleep centers at sleepeducation.org/find-a-facility.

- Searchable by state and city
- Filter for independent (non-hospital-system) facilities
- AASM-accredited labs are pre-qualified — they've invested in credentialing, which signals growth orientation
- Cross-reference against Apollo pull and dedupe before enrichment
- For non-AASM labs: NPI registry taxonomy code `261QS1200X` pulls sleep lab registrations by state

AASM accreditation signal: labs recently granted accreditation (visible via AASM directory additions) are a high-intent signal — use the "now that you're accredited, physicians will route to you — we help you make that introduction at scale" angle.

## Objection Handling (Quick Reference)

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We're at capacity" | "That's ideal timing — we build the referral network now so you have a warm bench when capacity opens. The worst outcome is too many physicians wanting to send you patients." |
| "Our physicians already know us" | "Your established physicians do. But new PCPs are entering your market every quarter and they don't have that history — we make sure they find you first before the hospital system does." |
| "We tried marketing before, it didn't work" | "Physician referral outreach isn't marketing — it's relationship development with a specific professional buyer. Very different from patient-facing ads or SEO. The message, channel, and targeting are completely different." |
| "We're owned by a hospital system" | "Understood — this would be for independent and physician-owned labs only. No hard feelings, good luck." |
