## AmSpa Directory Supplement

AmSpa (American Med Spa Association) maintains a member directory at americanmedspa.org. Members are owner-operators and medical directors — higher intent than a cold Apollo pull.

- Filter by state for geographic targeting
- Cross-reference against Apollo pull before enrichment — dedupe on company name + city
- AmSpa members are more likely to be growth-oriented (they paid for membership) → sequence can be slightly more direct

Also pull from:
- Alle (Allergan Aesthetics loyalty program) — aesthetics practices registered as Alle providers are verified injectors; provider list is publicly searchable at alle.com/find-a-provider
- Aspire (Galderma) — similar at galdermaskincare.com/find-a-provider

These supplement Apollo with verified active aesthetics practices.

## Objection Handling (Quick Reference)
