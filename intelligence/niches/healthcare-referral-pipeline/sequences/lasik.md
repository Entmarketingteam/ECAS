## State Optometry Board Supplement

State optometry boards publish full licensee lists — this is how you build the OD outreach list on behalf of LASIK clients (Phase 3 physician targeting).

Key state boards for OD pulls:
- California: optometry.ca.gov
- Texas: tob.texas.gov
- Florida: flhealthsource.gov
- New York: op.nysed.gov
- Illinois: idfpr.illinois.gov

Filter for: active license, OD designation (not optician), private practice / solo/small group (exclude LensCrafters, Walmart Vision, Target Optical).

NPI taxonomy code for optometrists: `152W00000X` — use NPI registry bulk pull by state + taxonomy for complete lists.

Dedupe against Apollo before enrichment. The highest-value ODs are those in independent private practice — they have full referral autonomy, unlike employed ODs at retail chains.

## Objection Handling (Quick Reference)

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already have OD relationships" | "How many active co-management partners are you seeing cases from monthly? Most practices have 3–5 strong ones and a long tail of ODs who referred once and drifted. We expand and re-engage that tail." |
| "ODs come to us when they're ready" | "Some do — the ones in your network. The ODs who went to optometry school in the last 5 years and are building their practice right now have never heard your name. That's who we reach." |
| "We're cutting back on marketing spend" | "This isn't ad spend — it's a one-time outreach program to build relationships that send you cases for years. The ROI timeline is completely different from paid acquisition." |
| "We're part of a larger group / DSO" | "Understood — if the individual center has budget authority and wants to grow its own OD network, we can work at the center level. If everything flows through corporate, not the right fit." |
