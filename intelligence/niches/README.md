## Tier 1 — Launch Now

### OSHA Compliance Consulting (9.5/10)
- LTV: $36k–$120k | Margins: 62–78% | Retainer: $4.5k–$8.5k/mo | Cycle: 45–75 days
- Unfair edge: OSHA public citation database = live self-refreshing lead list
- 15,000+ firms, zero cold email, all referral-dependent
- Folder: `osha-compliance/`

### Compounding Pharmacy (9.0/10)
- LTV: $42k–$72k | Margins: 55–75% | Retainer: $3.5k–$6k/mo | Cycle: 45–60 days
- Angle: Connect pharmacies to prescribing physicians (not patients — regulatory-safe)
- GLP-1 urgency: cash-rich pharmacies racing to lock in prescriber relationships
- Folder: `compounding-pharmacy/`

### Tax Credit Consulting — R&D + Cost Seg (8.5/10)
- LTV: $120k–$400k (R&D) / $40k–$120k (cost seg) | Margins: 65–80% | Cycle: 30–60 days
- Always cash-hungry (contingency fees collect 6–18 months later)
- Target: CFO/controller at $10M+ manufacturers — trivially Apollo-buildable
- AVOID ERC-heavy firms (post-2024 moratorium risk)
- Folder: `tax-credit-consulting/`

### Fiber / Broadband Deployment Contractors (8.5/10)
- LTV: $24k–$72k | Margins: 55–70% | Retainer: $2.5k–$5k/mo | Cycle: 30–60 days
- $42B BEAD program releasing state-by-state NOW
- Mid-tier contractors ($5M–$50M) scrambling for BD — state award maps are public
- Apollo: NAICS 237130 + geography
- Folder: *(no dedicated folder yet — from infrastructure sprint)*

### Arc Flash / Electrical Safety Consulting (8.0/10)
- LTV: $18k–$60k | Margins: 65–80% | Retainer: $2k–$5k/mo | Cycle: 30–45 days
- NFPA 70E mandatory, recurring every 5 years or after major modifications
- OSHA citation database = free lead list
- Folder: *(from infrastructure sprint)*

### Asbestos / Lead / Mold Abatement (8.0/10)
- LTV: $18k–$54k | Margins: 55–72% | Retainer: $2k–$4.5k/mo | Cycle: 30–45 days
- AHERA/EPA/OSHA compliance-triggered, event-driven (renovation, sale, insurance)
- Zero cold outreach in market — all referral-dependent
- Folder: *(from infrastructure sprint)*

### Emergency Generator Installation & Service (8.0/10)
- LTV: $24k–$96k | Margins: 52–68% | Retainer: $2.5k–$6k/mo | Cycle: 30–60 days
- NFPA 110 annual testing mandated, recurring service contracts

## Tier 3-4 — Avoid for Cold Traffic

Niches where the buyer is a utility, government, developer, or enterprise procurement committee:
- Transmission / Distribution / Grid Modernization (utility procurement)
- Wind / Hydrogen / BESS / Microgrid EPC (committee decisions, 12–36 mo cycles)
- Pipeline Construction / Compressor Station (midstream procurement)
- Bridge / Highway / Dam / Airport / Rail / Port (government)
- Chemical Plant EPC (majors procurement)
- Aviation MRO (airline institutional procurement)
- Robotics / Automation (CAPEX committee, 6–18 mo cycles)
- Industrial IoT (enterprise, multi-stakeholder)
- Healthcare RCM (3–6 month committee cycle)
- Craft Spirits (too low LTV)
