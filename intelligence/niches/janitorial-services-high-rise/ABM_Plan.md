# 1:1 ABM Strategy: High-Rise Janitorial Infrastructure
**Target:** Property Managers of Class A Office Buildings
**Focus Metro:** Chicago (The Loop & West Loop)
**Agency Role:** Local Infrastructure Partner (ContractMotion)

---

## 1. Target Account List (TAL) Criteria
We are not targeting "buildings"; we are targeting **Asset Value Preservation**. Our TAL is filtered by the following high-intent signals:

*   **BOMA EER Benchmarking (The Efficiency Gap):** Accounts spending >$1.85 PSF on cleaning (Chicago Class A average) without a corresponding "Elite" tenant satisfaction score.
*   **TI Permit Velocity (The Transition Signal):** Buildings with >3 active Tenant Improvement (TI) permits. New construction/build-outs are the highest risk for cleaning friction and the best entry point for a "Zero-Day Clean" narrative.
*   **LEED/WELL Certification Gaps:** Buildings that have "Silver" LEED status but are missing "WELL" Health-Safety Ratings. We position our janitorial protocols as the final mile to bridge the certification gap.
*   **Asset Profile:** 250,000+ SF, Class A, Multi-tenant occupancy with high-touch lobbies.

---

## 2. 'Vibe-Led' Creative Hook: "The Invisible Floor"
**The Concept:** In a Class A building, janitorial services should be like high-end infrastructure—silent, powerful, and invisible. When you notice the floor, it’s already a failure.

**The Creative Execution:** 
*   **Direct Mail Asset:** A high-end, matte-black "Building Health Audit" box containing a literal clear acrylic tile (representing the 'Invisible Floor') and a custom 4-page report benchmarking their building's PSF costs against BOMA EER regional data.
*   **Copy Hook:** *"Your tenants pay for the view, but they stay for the Invisible Floor. We manage the infrastructure that keeps your asset value from leaking out through the lobby."*

---

## 3. 4-Week Multi-Channel Sequence

### Week 1: The Benchmarking Breakthrough (Direct Mail)
*   **Action:** Send "The Invisible Floor" Audit Box via local courier (not FedEx/UPS).
*   **Content:** Personalized BOMA EER cost analysis vs. their estimated spend.
*   **Goal:** Establish immediate authority through data transparency.

### Week 2: The Neighborhood Hero Narrative (LinkedIn)
*   **Action:** 1:1 LinkedIn Connection + Narrative Ad sequence.
*   **Content:** Share a case study: *"How [Local Chicago Building] stabilized tenant retention during a $5M TI Build-out."* 
*   **Narrative:** Focus on being a "Chicago Infrastructure Partner." Highlight our local dispatch speed and knowledge of Chicago building codes/union dynamics.

### Week 3: The "Building Health" Roundtable (Local Event)
*   **Action:** Personal invitation to an exclusive breakfast at *The Soho House* or *The Metropolitan Club*.
*   **Focus:** A 45-minute "Class A Asset Preservation" roundtable with a LEED/WELL certification expert.
*   **Vibe:** No sales deck. Just 10 Property Managers discussing the "Post-Occupancy Reality" of 2026.

### Week 4: The "Digital Moat" Final Touch (Direct Outreach)
*   **Action:** Phone/Email follow-up offering a "Frictionless Transition Roadmap."
*   **Offer:** We provide a 30-day "Shadow Audit" where we analyze their current TI cleaning overlaps and LEED gaps for free, with no obligation to switch vendors.

---

## 4. 'Neighborhood Hero' Narrative: The Local Infrastructure Partner
We are moving away from the "Vendor" label. A vendor is a commodity; a partner is a pillar of the building’s ecosystem.

**The Narrative Pillars:**
1.  **Stewardship over Service:** "We don't just mop floors; we protect the capital investment of your ownership group."
2.  **Chicago-First Dispatch:** "We aren't a national roll-up. We are blocks away, part of the same skyline, and we understand that a spill in the West Loop is an emergency for your brand."
3.  **The WELL Bridge:** "Most janitorial firms use chemicals that kill your WELL certification. We provide the documentation and protocols that make your building the healthiest in the zip code."
4.  **Invisible Excellence:** "The highest compliment a Property Manager can receive is that the building feels 'effortless.' We are the engine of that effortlessness."
