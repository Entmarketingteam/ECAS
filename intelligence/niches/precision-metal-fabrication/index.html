Executive Summary

The US precision metal fabrication and CNC machining industry comprises 75,000+ machine shops generating over $420 billion annually. These businesses — ranging from 5-person Swiss screw machine shops to 200-employee aerospace parts manufacturers — share a near-universal blind spot: they get almost all their work through existing customer relationships and manufacturer's reps, doing zero proactive outbound.

When a customer diversifies away, they scramble. The opportunity for a cold email lead generation agency is substantial: help owners/presidents send targeted cold email to procurement managers, supply chain engineers, and mechanical engineers at OEM manufacturers actively qualifying new vendors.

The retainer model ($3,500–$6,500/month) fits the niche's cash flow profile perfectly, and the decision-maker is the owner or president — findable on LinkedIn with Apollo. Main objections are smokescreens for never having tried outbound.

Cash Flow Patterns

  • Payment terms: Net 30–60 days after invoice. Some OEM customers push Net 90.
  • Capital intensity: High — CNC machines cost $100K–$1M+ each. Shops carry significant debt service.

Financial Qualification Questions

  • "What percentage of your revenue comes from your top three customers right now?"
  • "Do you have a dedicated salesperson, or does BD fall on the owner/president?"
  • "When's the last time you landed a net-new customer relationship?"
  • "If one of your top customers shifted 30% of their volume tomorrow, what happens?"

Cultural Landmines

  • Don't call it a "factory." Machine shops are "shops" — always.
  • Don't mention social media strategy early. These shops don't want to be on TikTok. Lead with meetings.
  • Don't confuse "fabrication" with "machining." Conflating them signals ignorance.
  • Never promise results in "website traffic" or "impressions." They measure in RFQs and new customer relationships.
  • Never suggest they need to "build a brand." They need RFQs next quarter.

Part 10: Pain Points and Deeper Business Challenges

  • Customer concentration: "I've got three customers that represent 75% of my revenue. I lay awake knowing if one of them moves their supply chain, I'm in serious trouble." → Marketing solution: Diversify the customer base.
  • Feast or famine capacity: "Last quarter we were running three shifts. This quarter we've got two machines sitting idle." → Build consistent RFQ pipeline for backlog smoothing.
  • Commodity pricing pressure: "We keep losing work to shops in Mexico on price. I need customers who value quality." → Target buyers who have had quality problems offshore and are re-shoring.
  • No sales infrastructure: "I'm the best machinist in the building, but I'm also the salesperson, the estimator, and the HR department." → We do all prospecting; owner only shows up when there's a real conversation.
  • Exit valuation: "I want to sell this business in five years and get paid what it's worth." → A diversified, documented customer pipeline increases business value and reduces key-man dependence.
  • Skilled labor competition: "Everyone's fighting over the same 50 CNC operators in this market." → Strong pipeline = stable employment = better ability to recruit and retain operators.

Lead niche: General CNC Machining Job Shops and Swiss CNC Screw Machine Shops represent the largest addressable market with the clearest decision-maker profile (owner/president, $3M–$50M revenue, zero outbound activity). These are the initial ICP for campaign development.

Premium tier: Aerospace Machined Parts Manufacturers (AS9100), Medical Device Component Manufacturers (ISO 13485), and Semiconductor Equipment Parts Manufacturers command premium retainers ($6,000–$8,000/month) due to the specificity of their buyer universe and the urgency of re-shoring.

Core positioning: ContractMotion is not a marketing agency — we are a business development engine for manufacturers who have never done outbound. The language is always "qualified conversations," "procurement meetings," and "new customer relationships" — never "brand awareness," "impressions," or "traffic."

Outreach strategy: Apollo + LinkedIn to identify shop owners/presidents. Initial list: 500 targets per month. 3-touch email sequence. Reply handling in-house. Meeting booking routed directly to owner calendar. Monthly reporting call.
