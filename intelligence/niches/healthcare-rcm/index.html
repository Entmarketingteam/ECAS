Key Objections & Scripts
"Our clients come through referrals — cold email won't work in healthcare."
That's why there's opportunity. Every RCM firm is waiting for referrals — no one is owning outbound. One new health system client per year from outreach pays 3–5 years of our retainer. The math works at a 1% conversion rate.
"HIPAA makes outreach complicated."
HIPAA governs protected health information — it has zero bearing on B2B outreach to hospital executives about operational services. This misconception is exactly why your competitors aren't running outbound.
"We tried cold email once and got zero response."
Generic cold email in healthcare fails because it doesn't speak the language. Our sequences open with DNFB benchmarks, denial rate data by specialty, and RAC audit trends. A CFO recognizes immediately this isn't spam — it's someone who understands their world.
"We can't damage our reputation with health system contacts."
Every template goes through your review before launch. We target only organizations you specify. We never touch contacts you already have. Your reputation is the asset — we protect it with the same obsession you do.
Industry Vocabulary (Must Know)

DNFB

Discharged Not Final Billed — lagging revenue indicator. CFO pain point. Use in email subject lines.

Denial Rate

% of claims initially denied. Target <5%; >10% = crisis. Your email anchor metric.

RAC

Recovery Audit Contractor — CMS-appointed post-payment auditors. Triggers demand for defense consulting.

HCC Coding

Hierarchical Condition Category — risk adjustment for Medicare Advantage. Fastest-growing coding specialty.

CDI

Clinical Documentation Improvement — coding accuracy initiative. Major consulting service line for RCM firms.

340B

Federal drug pricing program for safety-net providers. Specialty consulting area with regulatory complexity.
