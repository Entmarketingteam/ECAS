Executive Summary

The global Aviation MRO market exceeds $100 billion annually and grows at 4-5% CAGR. Over 4,700 FAA-certificated repair stations operate in the United States alone. Despite operating in a sophisticated, safety-critical industry, the vast majority of MRO companies have virtually no outbound sales function — relying entirely on airline contracts, OEM referral networks, and word-of-mouth. This creates a compelling, under-served opportunity for a cold email lead generation agency connecting MRO shops with fleet operators, charter companies, and cargo carriers.

The core insight: MRO shop owners are ex-mechanics and ex-military pilots who built exceptional technical capabilities. They have never been approached with a credible, ROI-framed outbound sales system. The first agency to build this motion wins a multi-year retainer relationship worth $45K–$80K/year per client.

Top Objections & Responses

ObjectionReframe
"We're already at capacity." Capacity fills and empties. Build the pipeline now before you need it — your insurance policy against a slow quarter.
"Airlines have preferred vendor lists." Charter ops, cargo subcontractors, and corporate flight departments don't operate the same way. We target them first.
"We tried marketing — it didn't work." SEO and ads don't work for MRO because operators don't Google for maintenance. We go direct to named decision-makers.
"How would you know what to say to a DOM?" We research their fleet type, base locations, and aircraft count. The message references their specific operation — not a generic pitch.

Key Industry Vocabulary (Must Know Before Calling)

TAT
Turnaround Time — critical metric, shorter = aircraft back in service faster
AOG
Aircraft on Ground — emergency priority, every hour costs the operator revenue
Part 145
FAA Repair Station Certificate — always reference this for credibility
C-Check
Heavy maintenance cycle (~6,000-8,000 flight hours) — mid-level overhaul
A&P
Airframe & Powerplant mechanic — core FAA certification for maintenance workers
PMA Parts
FAA-approved aftermarket alternatives to OEM parts — 20-60% cost savings
CAMO
Continuing Airworthiness Management Organization — manages maintenance programs
AD
Airworthiness Directive — FAA-mandated maintenance, compliance is non-negotiable

Cultural landmine: Never say "fix planes" — say "maintain," "overhaul," or "service." Never call it "marketing" in your pitch — call it "business development" or "customer acquisition." Aviation MRO culture views marketing as something consumer brands do, not serious technical companies.
