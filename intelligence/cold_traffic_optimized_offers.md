# Cold-Traffic Optimized GTM Outreach Swipe File
## Daniel Fazio (Cold Email Wizard) Direct-Response Framework
**Status:** Operational | **Targeting:** Blue-Ocean Niches (Sales Nav Penetration < 15%)
**Core Copywriting Mandates:**
1.  **Monospace & Monosyllable:** Write like a peer, not a marketer. No capitalization in subject lines. Max 100 words.
2.  **No Filler:** Zero "hope you are well" or "we are a full-service agency."
3.  **Low-Friction CTAs:** Never ask for a 30-minute calendar block. Ask to send a Loom video, a list, or a PDF.
4.  **Immediate Signal Hook:** First line must prove you did not buy a list (using our Doppler scraper outputs).
5.  **Extreme Risk Reversal:** Pay-per-meeting, pay-per-qualified-lead, or a 100% money-back performance guarantee.
